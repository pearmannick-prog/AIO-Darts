About AIO Darts

An all-in-one darts app: pass-and-play scoring at one board, and online 1v1 over a direct connection with optional camera and mic so you can watch your opponent throw.

Three equal ways to score, and they all run through the same rules: a Granboard over Bluetooth, the clickable dartboard, or manual entry. A plain steel-tip board works as well as an electronic one.

x01 (301/501/701, every in and out rule), Cricket, Count Up and Bermuda Triangle, singly or as a medley. Solo play works in all of them, and every seat can be a computer player.

Online matches are peer-to-peer. Darts never touch a server: both browsers run the identical rules and stay in step, so a match cannot be interrupted by anything happening here.

Statistics

Optional. Guests play exactly as they always have, and everything is computed on-device from the darts you threw - an account only adds somewhere to keep it. Leaderboards rank self-reported scores: a peer-to-peer app with no referee cannot prove a match happened, and appearing on them is opt-in.

Rooms

Rooms say what you want to play, and on what. Stand in one and only the people in there with you can challenge you — tick “Open to challenges” if you want the whole lobby to be able to as well.
